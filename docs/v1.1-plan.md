# v1.1 plan

v1.0 missed three held-out targets: unanswerable declined 2/4 (target 0.80), Recall@10 0.833
(0.85) and citation precision 0.614 (0.90). Wrong declines (0.095) and unsupported-number
answers (0) met theirs. Results: `benchmarks/eval-all-2026-11-06-heldout-v1.json`,
`benchmarks/retrieval-all-2026-11-06-heldout-v1.json`. Diagnosis: `docs/decisions.tsv`,
rows tagged `v1-heldout`.

The v1.0 held-out set (`evals/heldout.jsonl`) has been seen and is retired. Nothing below is
tuned or re-scored against it. Every change is measured on dev, one at a time, kept or
reverted by the numbers and logged, as in v1.0.

## 1. Act on period flags

**Problem.** The period check flags a period or date that matches neither the cited filing
nor its text, but only annotates the answer. `heldout-unanswerable-002` gave Meta's
third-quarter 2026 Reality Labs loss as the fourth quarter; the flag fired and the sentence
still went out.

**Change.** Remove or rewrite a flagged sentence, the way sentences with unsupported numbers
are removed; abstain when nothing remains.

**Test cases.** Unit tests for the Meta case and for `heldout-lookup-008`, a false positive:
Micron's text says "for 2026 from 40% for 2025", and the answer's "fiscal 2025" was correct
but flagged. Measure false declines and unanswerable accuracy on dev before keeping it.

## 2. Grade "not reported; latest figure is X" as a decline

**Problem.** `heldout-unanswerable-003` asked for Lam's headcount at December 31, 2026. The
answer gave the dated figures the filings have and didn't claim a December figure, which the
item's review note allows. Abstention accuracy counts any answer as a failure.

**Change.** An answer to an unanswerable question passes if it says the asked-for value isn't
reported and gives only dated figures that it doesn't present as the asked-for period. Grade
this with a rule plus a judge check, and spot-check the judge by hand.

## 3. Checked alternate spans for restated facts

**Problem.** Correct citations of the same fact in another section score 0 (`heldout-lookup-003`:
the notes repeat the MD&A sentence; `heldout-lookup-004`: Part II Item 2 repeats it).

**Change.** Draft alternate spans for facts restated across sections, in the golden set and
the new held-out set, for the owner to check. `scripts/mark_checked.py` needs an option to
mark alternates.

## 4. Comparison retrieval

**Problem.** Comparison questions are the weakest type: held-out Recall@10 0.167 (3
questions), dev 0.417. The older filing's passage ranks low inside its own filing. Ranking by
how much text changed was measured and rejected in v1.0
(`benchmarks/novelty-dev-2026-11-06.txt`).

**Investigate on dev.** Per-side queries (rewrite the question for each filing), a larger
per-filing candidate pool for comparisons, and a compare step that writes one sentence
across the per-part answers.

## 5. A new held-out set

**Why.** The v1.0 set is seen, and with 4 unanswerable and 3 comparison questions the floor
and the comparison numbers were too small to read: the 0.80 floor needed 4 of 4.

**Plan.** Draft a new set after changes 1 to 4 are kept, from filings indexed after those
changes where possible, with 8 to 10 unanswerable and 8 to 10 comparison questions among the
rest. Every question names its period. The owner checks it before its one logged run.

## Also open from v1.0

- `unanswerable-008` (test split) became answerable after the November re-ingest.
- 68 golden questions and 8 alternate spans are unchecked.
- A figure can come from the wrong duration (`heldout-lookup-009`: nine-month capital
  expenditures reported as the quarter's). Neither check catches it; a duration check against
  XBRL facts is one option to test.
