Six read-only tools for any MCP client

Claude Desktop, the MCP Inspector, or your own client. Arguments are JSON.

answer_question
A cited answer or an abstention, with the passages used.
{"question": "Does Micron's fiscal 2025 10-K list customer concentration as a risk factor?", "tickers": ["MU"]}
search_filings
Ranked passages with ticker, form, period, section and offsets.
{"query": "export controls China", "tickers": ["AMAT"], "k": 3}
get_financial_fact
An exact XBRL value with unit, period, duration and source filing. Nothing is computed.
{"ticker": "AMD", "concept": "revenue", "fiscal_year": 2026, "fiscal_period": "Q3"}
compare_filings
One section of two filings, by topic or by what changed most. Works across a 10-K and a 10-Q.
{"ticker": "MSFT", "section": "1A", "k": 3}
get_passage
An exact span by accession, section and offsets, with surrounding text and the EDGAR link.
{"accession": "0001045810-26-000075", "section": "I-2", "start_char": 17134, "end_char": 17279}
list_coverage
Indexed companies, filing counts, newest filing per company and when the index was last updated.

How an answer is built

  1. FetchA polite EDGAR client stores each filing once, at most 6 requests a second.
  2. SectionHTML becomes text, split on Item headings such as 1A and 7.
  3. IndexAbout 500-token chunks in Postgres full-text and pgvector.
  4. RetrieveBoth searches merged by reciprocal rank fusion, scoped to the filings the question names.
  5. Answerqwen3:8b answers each part separately, and every sentence must cite a passage.
  6. CheckUncited sentences and numbers missing from their passage are removed. Wrong periods are flagged.

What it doesn't do yet

  • No 20-F filers. TSM, ASML and ARM aren't covered.
  • Comparisons sit side by side. Each filing is answered on its own, and the answer doesn't compare them.
  • A figure can carry the wrong duration. One held-out answer gave a nine-month total as the quarter's.
  • The model is small. qwen3:8b wrongly declined 2 of 21 answerable held-out questions.
  • Most eval questions are unchecked. Only the 25 held-out questions were checked by hand.

One script from clone to server

Needs Python 3.11+, Docker Desktop and Ollama, all running, and about 7 GB of disk. The setup script indexes 96 filings, loads XBRL figures and checks every dependency. The first run takes about six minutes on a machine with a GPU, plus downloads. Rerunning it is safe.

Then filings mcp-config prints the Claude Desktop entry for your machine.

git clone https://github.com/ixtaqq/goldirham-filings.git
cd goldirham-filings
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts\setup.ps1
